## Core Philosophy: The 3-Stage Mastery Pipeline

FluentEdge enforces a structured, step-by-step pedagogical progression. Learners cannot simply jump ahead into spoken delivery without first internalizing academic vocabulary and producing a cohesive, syntactically rich essay that passes automated rubric gates:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ Stage 1: Vocabulary & Phonology │  10 Compulsory Academic Words (3 Verbs, 3 Nouns, 2 Adjectives, 2 Adverbs)
│                                 │  IPA Transcriptions, RP Audio Synthesis, Collocations & Formal Definitions
└────────────────┬────────────────┘
                 │ (Candidate reviews lexis & proceeds)
                 ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ Stage 2: Composition & Discourse │  Hierarchical Tree Topics (36 Root Subjects × 26 Sub-Themes × Tiers)
│                                 │  Obligatory Focus & Topic Adherence Gatekeeper
│                                 │  100% Compulsory Lexis Guard (10/10 fulfillment required)
│                                 │  Strict Word Count (±5 leniency) & Paragraph Architecture Gates
│                                 │  Live Syntax Radar (14 Inversion & Complex Grammatical Patterns)
│                                 │  Automated 4-Scale CEFR Evaluator (Content, Register, Org, Language)
└────────────────┬────────────────┘
                 │ (Passes Band 4 / C1 or Band 5 / C2 threshold)
                 ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ Stage 3: Speaking Studio        │  Real-Time Teleprompter with Target Essay Alignment
│                                 │  Live Web Speech Recognition (phonetic & token-by-token feedback)
│                                 │  HTML5 Audio Spectrum Visualizer & British RP Model Audio
│                                 │  Speaking Performance Report (Accuracy %, Cadence WPM, CEFR Band)
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Detailed Features

### 1. Stage 1: Vocabulary & Phonaesthetics Studio

Before drafting an essay, candidates are equipped with an expertly curated 10-word academic lexicon:

* **Balanced Lexical Profile**: Exactly **3 Verbs**, **3 Nouns**, **2 Adjectives**, and **2 Adverbs** sampled from authentic C1/C2 academic corpora.
* **Linguistic Depth**:
  * International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) transcriptions.
  * Formal dictionary definitions tailored to scholarly registers.
  * Natural academic collocations (e.g., *precipitous decline*, *undermine democratic legitimacy*, *exacerbate systemic inequality*).
  * Native British English (Received Pronunciation - RP) audio synthesis button to listen to authentic pronunciation.
* **Educational Requirements Dashboard**: Dynamic display detailing active CEFR level criteria, minimum word counts, mandatory paragraph thresholds, and required syntax structures before drafting begins.

---

### 2. Stage 2: Composition Studio & Discourse Architecture

#### A. Tree Topic Architecture
Instead of static, repetitive prompts, FluentEdge utilizes a procedural combinatorial **Tree Architecture**:
* **Root Subject**: 36 comprehensive academic domains covering technology, governance, ethics, philosophy, urbanism, environment, and social dynamics.
* **Two Sub-Themes**: 26 thematic lenses branching from the root subject to form nuanced essay questions.
* **Tension & Scope**: Integrated philosophical tension (e.g., *Individual Freedom vs Collective Welfare*, *Innovation vs Regulation*) and societal scope (e.g., *at the individual level*, *for nation-states*).
* **Tiered Difficulty Filter (`Alt+D`)**:
  * **Easy (Tier 1)**: Relatable, grounded everyday topics (*Social Media & Daily Life*, *Remote Work*, *Urban Transportation*).
  * **Medium (Tier 2)**: Structural and institutional analyses (*Food Security*, *Tertiary Education*, *Monopolies*).
  * **Hard (Tier 3)**: Complex epistemic and existential inquiries (*Epistemic Authority*, *Neurotechnology*, *Transhumanism*).
  * **All Difficulties**: Unrestricted full-pool generation.
* **Default Starting Topic**: A carefully selected, accessible Easy-tier topic (*Social Media & Daily Life* with *Personal Growth & Self-Improvement* and *Creativity & Innovation*) to provide an inviting entry point without political controversy.
* **Instant Topic Re-roll (`Alt+T`)**: Draw new combinations anytime respecting the active difficulty filter.
* **Professional Tech Topic Engine (`Alt+K`)**: Draw exclusive workplace scenarios and engineering dilemmas tailored to the tech industry (e.g., *Microservices vs Monolith*, *Technical Debt Modernization*, *Zero-Trust Security*, *Enterprise LLM Guardrails*, *Cloud FinOps*, *CI/CD Pipelines*) with authentic engineering prompts (Architectural Decision Records, executive leadership memos, and post-mortem mitigation strategies).

#### B. Obligatory Focus & Topic Adherence Gatekeeper
Candidates cannot bypass the essay topic or write generic boilerplate text. The natural language evaluator verifies:
1. Meaningful engagement with the **Root Subject** (minimum 2 key domain terms/concepts).
2. Meaningful engagement with **Sub-Theme 1** (minimum 1 thematic concept).
3. Meaningful engagement with **Sub-Theme 2** (minimum 1 thematic concept).
If an essay drifts off-topic, the assessment gatekeeper blocks progression and highlights which thematic nodes are missing.

#### C. Obligatory Word Count & Paragraph Gates (with ±5 Leniency)
To reflect authentic academic examination regulations, essays must strictly comply with word and paragraph thresholds:

| Standard | Target Word Count | Permitted Word Range (±5 Leniency) | Obligatory Paragraph Count | Discourse Structure |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **C1 Advanced** | 220–260 words | **215–265 words** | **3 to 4 paragraphs** | Intro, Analytical Body (1-2), Conclusion |
| **C2 Proficiency** | 280–320 words | **275–325 words** | **4 to 5 paragraphs** | Intro, Dual Opposing Angles, Synthesis, Conclusion |

*Any essay with fewer or more words, or with an invalid paragraph count, is strictly gated with actionable diagnostic feedback.*

#### D. 100% Compulsory Target Lexis Guard
Every single one of the **10 vocabulary words** generated in Stage 1 must be seamlessly integrated into the essay (10/10 fulfillment).
* **Inflection & Stem Recognition**: Smart lemmatization detects inflected forms (e.g., *mitigate* is recognized when writing *mitigating*, *mitigated*, or *mitigation*).
* **Live Vocabulary Dock**: A persistent floating dock on Stage 2 displays color-coded chips that turn green with a `✓` checkmark as words are typed, accompanied by a real-time counter (`X/10 Words Used`).

#### E. Real-Time Syntax Radar (14 Complex Structures)
FluentEdge continuously scans the essay for 14 distinctive C1/C2 syntactic patterns:
1. **Negative / Limiting Inversion**: *Seldom has...*, *Rarely do...*, *Under no circumstances...*
2. **Cleft / Focus Structure**: *What remains of paramount concern is...*, *It was the rapid proliferation that...*
3. **Passive Reporting Clause**: *It is widely contended that...*, *It has long been maintained that...*
4. **Inverted Conditional (without "if")**: *Were governments to hesitate...*, *Had regulators intervened earlier...*, *Should authorities...*
5. **Advanced Concession / Contrast**: *Notwithstanding*, *Albeit*, *Inasmuch as*, *Regardless of*
6. **Fronted Participle / Gerund Clause**: *Having examined the empirical data,...*, *Confronted with systemic failure,...*
7. **Mandative / Formulaic Subjunctive**: *It is imperative that the commission be impartial...*, *recommend that it remain...*
8. **Nominative Absolute Clause**: *The conference having concluded, delegates drafted...*, *All factors considered,...*
9. **Mixed Unreal Conditional**: *Had authorities acted a decade ago, contemporary democracy would not now be vulnerable...*
10. **Complex Prepositional / Formal Relative**: *a dilemma the ramifications of which extend...*, *mechanisms whereby...*
11. **Correlative / Proportional Comparative**: *The more interconnected platforms become, the greater the potential risk...*
12. **Inverted Concession with Though / As**: *Arduous though it may be,...*, *Complex as this reality is,...*
13. **Fronted Adverbial / Prepositional Locator**: *Central to this debate lies...*, *Among these variables stands...*
14. **Nominalised Rhetorical Subject**: *The relentless commodification of knowledge...*

#### F. Precision AI Prompt Generator (`Alt+P`)
For learners wanting to observe model essays or rapidly test speech integration, a single click generates an exact, heavily-constrained system prompt tailored for LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini):
* Injects the active CEFR standard, exact word boundaries (with ±5 leniency), and paragraph count requirements.
* Injects the Root Subject, Sub-Themes, and Prompt Directive.
* Embeds all 10 compulsory vocabulary items with their grammatical parts of speech.
* Requires the inclusion of designated syntax radar patterns.
* Enforces strict raw-text output (no conversational commentary, titles, or bullet points).
* Includes an interactive inspection modal with interactive copy button.

#### G. Practice Assessment Rubric
Submissions are scored on an authentic 20-point CEFR scale (four 5-point categories):
* **Content (0–5)**: Word count adherence, prompt topic fidelity, and developmental depth.
* **Communicative Achievement (0–5)**: Scholarly register, academic tone, avoidance of colloquialisms or informal contractions.
* **Organisation (0–5)**: Logical paragraphing, signposting markers, cohesive devices, and synthesis architecture.
* **Language (0–5)**: Lexical sophistication, 100% compulsory vocabulary coverage, and syntactic variety across the 14 syntax patterns.

### 3. Stage 3: Speaking & Phonetics Studio

Once an essay achieves a passing score (**Band 4 / C1** or **Band 5 / C2**), Stage 3 unlocks automatically:

* **Neural Speech Engine (Kokoro TTS)**:
  * Powered by Kokoro-82M ONNX model running entirely in the browser via WebAssembly (WASM).
  * High-fidelity, natural, human-like neural intonation and phonological precision.
  * **Multi-Voice & Accent Selector (4 Specially Tuned Options)**:
    * **🇬🇧 UK English (British RP)**:
      * ♀ **Female**: `Isabella` (`bf_isabella` — feminine, velvety, and mellow)
      * ♂ **Male**: `Fable` (`bm_fable` — calm, deep British narrative baritone)
    * **🇺🇸 USA English (General American)**:
      * ♀ **Female**: `Heart` (`af_heart` — Kokoro flagship Grade-A warm, feminine, mellow voice)
      * ♂ **Male**: `Michael` (`am_michael` — calm, deep, clear American baritone without bass boominess)
  * **Real-time Acoustic DSP Equalization**: Web Audio biquad filter chain applies custom curves:
    * *Female Profile*: De-harshness at 2.8 kHz with a silky 5.8 kHz air shelf for a gentle, mellow presence.
    * *Male Profile*: 175 Hz chest resonance for depth and calm authority, coupled with an 85 Hz high-pass filter that eliminates muddiness and boom.
  * **Header Flag Bar**: Placed prominently directly beneath the application header, featuring 2 national flag cards divided into Female and Male buttons for instantaneous switching.
  * **Real-time Voice Preview**: Test any selected voice with an immediate phonological sample.
  * **Seamless Dual-Engine Fallback**: Operates with browser SpeechSynthesis when offline or running under strict local `file://` sandboxes, ensuring 100% audio uptime.
* **Synchronized Teleprompter**: Displays the written essay in large, high-legibility typography designed for oral presentation, with word-by-word tracking.
* **Live Speech Recognition (Web Speech API)**:
  * Microphone stream processes speech in real time with recognition language automatically synchronized to the selected accent (`en-GB` or `en-US`).
  * Word-level phonetic matching highlights spoken words in **Green** (accurate), **Amber** (minor phonetic variation), or **Red** (omitted/unclear).
* **HTML5 Audio Visualizer**: Live waveform oscilloscope rendered on a 60fps canvas displaying microphone input dynamics.
* **Speaking Performance Report**:
  * **Pronunciation Accuracy (%)**: Token match accuracy against target text.
  * **Speaking Cadence (WPM)**: Words-per-minute tracked against the scholarly 130–160 WPM fluent presentation benchmark.
  * **Estimated CEFR Speaking Band**: Diagnostic score combining accuracy and rhythm.

## Technical Architecture

* **100% Client-Side Runtime**: No backend server, external API keys, or cloud databases required. Everything runs locally in the browser.
* **Modern Vanilla Stack**:
  * **HTML5**: Semantic, accessible markup.
  * **CSS3**: Custom Prestige Dark Glassmorphism design system using HSL color tokens, backdrop filters, and CSS variables.
  * **JavaScript (ES6+)**: Modular architecture (`js/app.js`, `js/data/topics.js`, `js/data/vocabulary.js`, `js/modules/evaluator.js`, `js/modules/speech.js`).
* **Web Standards APIs**:
  * `Web Speech API` (`SpeechRecognition` / `webkitSpeechRecognition`) for live voice recognition.
  * `Web Speech API` (`SpeechSynthesis`) for British English model pronunciation.
  * `Web Audio API` (`AudioContext`, `AnalyserNode`) for live 60fps audio waveform visualizer.
  * `LocalStorage` for persisting evaluation reports and selected CEFR levels across sessions.
* **Self-Contained Bundle**: A bundled distribution (`js/bundle.js`) compiled via `build_bundle.ps1` allows the application to be opened directly as a static file (`index.html`) or served over static hosting without Node.js dependencies.
