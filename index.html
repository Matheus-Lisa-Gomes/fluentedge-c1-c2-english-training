Technical Architecture & Assessment Workflow

How You Are Evaluated in Stages 2 & 3

A comprehensive technical breakdown of analytical scoring, writing stage flexibility, and oral acoustic measurement.

Stage 2 Analytical Assessment

Writing Studio: 4 Core Evaluation Scales (20 Pts)

Your essay is evaluated via a multi-dimensional rubric assessing four analytical parameters (0–5 marks each) to calculate an aggregate score and overall band:

Content (0–5): Complete prompt fulfillment, nuanced thesis formulation, in-depth analytical argument.
Communicative Achievement (0–5): Academic tone, third-person stance, epistemic hedging, zero informal shortcuts/contractions.
Organisation (0–5): Cohesive discourse flow, logical paragraph architecture, sophisticated transitional devices.
Language (0–5): Sophisticated lexical collocation, morphological precision, complex clause structures.
Gatekeepers: 10/10 Compulsory Words + Minimum Radar Structures + ≥75% (C1) / ≥85% (C2) to unlock Stage 3

Stage 3 Acoustic Evaluation

Speaking Studio: Live Teleprompter & Cadence

In Stage 3, you read your evaluated essay aloud. The speech engine analyzes your delivery through three real-time dimensions:

Token Matching Accuracy

Speech-to-Text recognition verifies each word against your text in real time. Accurate tokens illuminate green as you pronounce them.

Natural Cadence (WPM Rate)

Monitors speaking pace against natural fluent delivery: 130–150 WPM (C1) and 140–165 WPM (C2) to eliminate unnatural pauses or rushed delivery.

Native British RP Model

Listen to an AI-synthesized Received Pronunciation audio model demonstrating authentic sentence stress, pitch modulation, and liaisons.
